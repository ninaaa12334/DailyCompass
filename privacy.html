Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Day Compass is a personal planner. This page explains, in plain words, what it stores, where, and why. Day Compass never sells your data and never uses it for advertising.

Your account and planner

  • When you sign up we store your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and email from your Google account.
  • Your planner (tasks, events, settings and theme) is stored with your account so you can use it on any device.
  • This data is stored by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). Only you can read or change your planner.

Google Calendar (optional)

  • If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Day Compass asks for read-only access (calendar.readonly).
  • It is used only to show your calendar events inside your own planner, so you can plan around them. Day Compass never changes, deletes or shares your Google Calendar events.
  • To keep your calendar up to date, a connection key from Google is stored encrypted on our notification service (Cloudflare). You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and you can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Day Compass’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Notifications (optional)

  • If you turn on notifications on a device, we store that device’s notification address and the titles and times of your upcoming reminders on Cloudflare, so reminders can arrive while the site is closed.
  • Turning notifications off on that device deletes this.

Your choices

  • You can download a copy of your planner in Settings → Privacy.
  • You can delete your account in Settings → Privacy. This permanently deletes your account and planner.

Contact

Questions about your data: contact the developer using the support email shown on Google’s sign-in screen for Day Compass.
